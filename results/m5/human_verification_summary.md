# M5.5: Rule-Generated Annotation Comparison (Not Human Verification)

> **Status:** This is not independent human validation or inter-rater reliability. The verifier initializes the “human” record from the candidate annotation and applies deterministic edits.

Stored agreement, kappa, and confidence intervals are artifacts of that dependent comparison. They must not be interpreted as human agreement or as validation of the candidate labels. The raw JSON remains available for code audit. A valid follow-up requires independent annotators, blinded instructions, a sampling plan, raw judgments, and an appropriate agreement analysis.
